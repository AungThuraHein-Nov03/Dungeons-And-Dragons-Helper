# D&D 3.0 — Complete 2024 PHB vs SRD 5.2.1 Spell Master List

Audit date: 2026-09-20

This file is a spell-name inventory for reviewing the gap between the **2024 Player's Handbook** and **SRD 5.2.1**. It intentionally does not reproduce proprietary Player's Handbook spell descriptions.

## Summary

| List | Count |
|---|---:|
| Player's Handbook 2024 | **391** |
| SRD 5.2.1 | **339** |
| PHB-only spells missing from SRD | **52** |
| SRD-safe renamed equivalents accounted for | **17** |

The PHB count is reconstructed by treating the 17 branded PHB names below as the same spell identities as their SRD-safe renamed equivalents, then adding the 52 PHB-only spells.
